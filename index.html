Module 1 • Fundamentals

Definition of High-Risk Neonate

1. A newborn (regardless of gestational age or birth weight) who has a greater than average chance of morbidity or mortality because of threats to life and health that occur during the prenatal, perinatal, and postnatal periods.

2. Any neonate exposed to any condition that makes their life in danger.

Predisposing Factors

    Gestational Age (GA)

    Definition: The course of time from day one of the menstrual cycle until birth.

    5 Methods of Determination:

    1. Physical & neurological exam
    2. Last Menstrual Period (L.M.P)
    3. Obstetric history
    4. Laboratory tests
    5. Fetal ultrasonic scanning

    Physical Identification: Preterm LBW vs. Term

    Anatomical Sign Preterm Neonate Term Neonate
    Breast Tissue & Nodule Flat areola, underdeveloped breast tissue, absent/tiny nodule Raised areola, well-formed breast tissue & palpable nodule
    Female Genitalia Prominent clitoris and labia minora; labia majora widely separated Labia majora fully developed, covering clitoris and labia minora
    Male Genitalia Undescended testes, smooth scrotum with few rugae Testes descended into pendulous scrotum covered with deep rugae
    Plantar Surface (Sole Creases) Smooth plantar surface with few or anterior-only creases Deep plantar creases covering the entire sole from toe to heel
